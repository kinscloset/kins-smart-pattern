Moonlit Pages

"Some stories are not written in daylight."

The Story

Some stories are not written in daylight. They appear quietly, somewhere between midnight thoughts and unfinished dreams.

This piece was inspired by those moments of solitude— when the world grows still, when the noise fades, and all that remains are the thoughts we have been carrying all along.

The openwork stitches echo the spaces between words, the pauses between chapters, the things left unsaid. Like pages illuminated by moonlight, this piece is gentle, reflective, and unhurried.

Under moonlight, everything feels softer. The world slows down. The mind wanders. And suddenly, what once felt hidden begins to make sense.

Moonlit Pages is an ode to those quiet hours— when we write our truest stories, even if no one else ever reads them.
